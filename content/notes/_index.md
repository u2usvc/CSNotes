## Why?

One day I had a thought that dumping parts of my personal notes to a public resource isn't that bad of an idea after all. That's how this website was born.

Documenting theory is usually error-prone and time-consuming.
So I've decided to avoid it and instead put out something that **just works** and let the reader figure the rest out.

That's easy enough for me to not get burned out and informative enough to actually be useful to somebody (I hope).
